About Catalyst Studios

A one-person studio, built around doing it properly.

Catalyst Studios was started in Limerick with a simple frustration: too many small businesses were being sold websites that looked fine on launch day and then quietly failed to bring in a single enquiry — because nobody had thought about SEO, speed, or mobile experience until it was too late.

I work directly with every client, from the first conversation to the day the site goes live. There's no account manager relaying messages to an offshore dev team — you're talking to the person actually building your site.

The name "Catalyst" is the point: a website should be the thing that speeds up growth for your business, not a box you tick and forget about.

What I believe

  • A website isn't finished until it can actually be found
  • Design should serve the business, not just look impressive
  • Clear communication beats jargon, every time
  • Fast, accessible sites aren't optional extras

Where I Work

Based in Limerick. Working across Ireland.

Every project runs smoothly over call and email, so location has never been a barrier — whether you're around the corner in the city, or elsewhere in Munster and beyond.
