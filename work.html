Our Work

Concept builds that show how we think.

Catalyst Studios is a newer studio, so instead of padding this page with someone else's stock template, here are three concept projects — each solving a real kind of business problem. Real client case studies will replace these as projects launch.

Concept

Restaurant & Hospitality · Concept

Riverside Bistro

An independent Limerick restaurant needs a site that makes reservations effortless and puts the menu and atmosphere front and centre.

The approach

A warm, dark palette with a single amber accent, large food-forward imagery placeholders, and a reservation button that follows you down the page. Menu and opening hours load fast for the "what time do they close" mobile search.

Focus areas

Local SEO for "restaurant near me" style searches, structured data for opening hours & menu, one-tap call and directions on mobile.

Tourism & Bookings · Concept

Aran Coastal Tours

A tour operator needs visitors to go from "just browsing" to "booked" in as few taps as possible — especially on a phone, standing on a pier.

The approach

Itinerary-led pages built around each tour, clear pricing and availability, and a sticky "Check Availability" call to action that never scrolls out of reach.

Focus areas

Fast image loading for scenic photography, seasonal content structure for SEO, and a booking flow that works as well on 4G as on wifi.

Professional Services · Concept

Forge & Co. Architects

An architecture studio's website is often its most important portfolio piece — it needs to feel considered, not busy.

The approach

A restrained grid layout that lets project photography do the talking, generous whitespace, and a typeface pairing that feels architectural rather than corporate.

Focus areas

Case-study style project pages for SEO depth, fast-loading galleries, and a simple enquiry form that qualifies leads before the first call.
