Questions

Common questions about the programme.

Commission is paid out within 30 days of the referred client's final project payment clearing. We'll contact you directly when that happens and arrange the payment — bank transfer, PayPal, or whatever works best for you.

A referral is successful when the business you introduced signs a project agreement with Catalyst Studios and completes payment. If a referral enquires but doesn't proceed, no commission is due — but we'll let you know either way so you're never left guessing.

No limit at all. Refer as many businesses as you like — you'll earn 25% on every one that converts. There's no minimum either, so there's no pressure if referrals come in slowly at first.

We're open to anyone with a genuine audience or network of businesses — freelancers, consultants, professionals, creators, or well-connected individuals. We review each application personally and will let you know within a few days of applying.
